## Create an account

Browsing, searching and test matings work without an account. To add cats you need one:

1. Open [Create account](/register/) and enter your name, email and a password of at least 12 characters.
2. We send a confirmation link to your email address. Open it once; until then you can sign in but not add cats.
3. Lost your password? Use *Forgot password* on the sign-in page and follow the emailed link.

## Find a cat

On the [Cats](/cats/) page type a name, or narrow the search by sex, breed code, EMS colour, year of birth or registration number. Tick *exact match* to match a whole name instead of part of it. Open a cat to see its details, its **coefficient of inbreeding**, its kittens and its pedigree.

## Add a cat

Open **Account → Add a cat**.

- **Name and sex** are required.
- **Sire and dam:** type at least two letters and pick the parent from the list. The sire list only shows males and the dam list only females. A cat cannot be its own ancestor, and a cat that already has kittens cannot change sex.
- Titles, breed, EMS colour, registration number, breeder, owner, chip number, photo link and notes are optional.

New cats start as **waiting for approval**. Only you and the administrators can see them until an administrator publishes them, and other users cannot choose them as parents. You can edit or delete your own cats at any time; editing a published cat keeps it published.

## Read a pedigree

The cat page draws the ancestors as a tree, with the sire above the dam. Males are tinted differently from females, and every box links to that cat. Branches that end in an *Unknown* box have no recorded parent.

## Test a mating

Open [Test mating](/test-mating/), choose a sire and a dam and press the button. The result is the coefficient of inbreeding an imaginary kitten of that pair would have, based on the recorded ancestors. Nothing is saved. See [how inbreeding is calculated](/docs/genetics/inbreeding/) for what the number means and its limits.

## Birthdays and language

[Birthdays](/birthdays/) lists cats whose birthday is today or coming up. The language button in the header switches between English and Slovenčina; you can also store your preferred language in your account.
